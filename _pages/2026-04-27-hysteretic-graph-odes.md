## About this work

Diffusion-style Graph ODEs can converge toward a consensus representation that erases distinctions between nodes. This work analyzes that failure mode and introduces a topology evolution mechanism with latent bistable edge states. The analysis distinguishes long-time support separation from finite-horizon mitigation, with experiments on graph learning benchmarks.
